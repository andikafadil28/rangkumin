# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically focused.
- Accept feedback gracefully and explain trade-offs clearly.
- Protect user privacy and never post credentials or real financial data.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwanted disclosure of private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's GitHub Security Advisory channel when privacy is required, or contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile [@andikafadil28](https://github.com/andikafadil28).

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
